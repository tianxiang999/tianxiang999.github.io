I received my Ph.D. degree of Computer Science from [University of Hong Kong](https://www.cs.hku.hk) in
2024, under the supervision of [Prof. Heming Cui](https://i.cs.hku.hk/~heming/).
My research interests are in systems and security. In my past Ph.D. career, I
mainly focused on using trusted hardware like Intel SGX to build secure and performant
distributed
computing systems (including data networking systems, distributed databases, and
secure AI serving systems). See my full list of publications [here](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=O9OOpR0AAAAJ&hl=zh-CN).

I review several top-tier (CCF-A) journals, such as TPDS, TDSC, JSAC, and ToN. I
was the web chair of APSys 2021. I was the Artifact Evaluation
Committee for SOSP 2021.

<strong><em style="color: red;">💥 Recently: I'm selected for the 10th Young
Elite Scientists Sponsorship Program, with 300,000RMB research grant, by China
Association For Science And Technology (国家级人才项目)</em></strong>
