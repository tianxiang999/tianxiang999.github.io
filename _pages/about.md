I am currently a fourth-year (2018--now) PhD student in Computer Science
Department at The University of Hong Kong. I am under the supervision of Prof.
Heming Cui [link](https://i.cs.hku.hk/~heming/). 

I am broadly interested in distributed systems, security and AI. I have been focusing on building practical trusted hardware-based
(e.g., SGX) systems to secure decentralized computing (e.g., Internet-scale
private messaging, blockchain) and AI training/inference.
